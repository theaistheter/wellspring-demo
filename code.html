General Health Information

1. Balanced Nutrition

General guidance includes eating a variety of fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and lean proteins, and drinking adequate water. Maintaining regular meal times can support energy levels.

Source: World Health Organization (WHO) - Healthy Diet Fact Sheet; Department of Health Philippines - Pinggang Pinoy Guide

2. Regular Physical Activity

WHO recommends that children and adolescents get an average of 60 minutes per day of moderate-to-vigorous physical activity. For adults, 150 minutes of moderate activity per week is a common general reference. Simple activities like walking, stretching, and play-based movement count.

Source: WHO Physical Activity Guidelines 2020; Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) - Physical Activity Basics

3. Mental Well-being and Sleep

General wellness includes getting 7-9 hours of quality sleep for adults and 8-10 hours for teenagers, taking regular breaks from screens, practicing deep breathing, and talking to trusted family, friends, or school counselors when stressed.

Source: CDC - About Sleep; WHO - Mental Health: Strengthening Our Response

Health Tips & Trustworthy Resources

  • Stay Hydrated: Bring a reusable water bottle to school.
  • Move Every Hour: Stand and stretch between study periods.
  • Practice Hand Hygiene: Wash hands with soap for at least 20 seconds.
  • Screen Time Break: Follow the 20-20-20 rule for eye rest.
